# Hermes Mobile

register: product

## Product purpose
An Android Flutter client for a private Hermes remote gateway over Tailscale. Users manage ongoing agent sessions, send attachments, steer work, queue follow-up prompts, and receive completion alerts.

## Users and scene
Users switch between phone apps and check long-running agent tasks, often on a dim phone screen. Preserve the warm dark interface rather than introducing a new visual direction.

## Established design constraints
White Arabic copy, RTL layout, English model identifiers and Thinking levels, Tabler icons with no emoji. Warm near-black surfaces, coral accent, rounded controls and restrained shadows. Show confirmations for costly or context-sensitive model switches; never accept them automatically. Short feedback is non-modal; confirmations requiring a decision may use a modal. Keep long text scrollable, actions visible, and layouts safe at 320 logical pixels and enlarged text.
